# Activation Versus Connectivity

## Segregation versus integration

Standard fMRI analyses focus on functional segregation: asking which individual brain regions activate during an experimental task. Functional connectivity investigates functional integration: asking how signals in different regions vary together. Two areas can show dramatic changes in functional coupling even when their mean activation levels remain unchanged.

## Connectivity as a statistical interaction

Psychophysiological interaction, or PPI, models functional connectivity within the general linear model as a statistical interaction. It tests whether the regression slope relating activity in a seed region to the rest of the brain changes with psychological context after modeling the main effects. A PPI association does not by itself establish causal communication.

## Connectivity concepts

Separate activation magnitude from inter-regional coupling. PPI tests context-dependent changes in statistical coupling. Changes can be positive or negative and do not establish causal influence.
