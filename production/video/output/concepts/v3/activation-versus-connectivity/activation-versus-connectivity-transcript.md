# Activation Versus Connectivity

## Segregation versus integration

Standard fMRI analyses focus on functional segregation: asking which individual brain regions activate during an experimental task. Functional connectivity investigates functional integration: asking how regions communicate as coordinated networks. Two areas can show dramatic changes in functional coupling even when their mean activation levels remain unchanged.

## Connectivity as a statistical interaction

Psychophysiological interaction, or PPI, models functional connectivity within the general linear model as a statistical interaction. It tests whether the regression slope relating activity in a seed region to the rest of the brain significantly increases during a specific psychological condition.

## Connectivity concepts

Separate activation magnitude from inter-regional coupling. PPI provides a statistical framework to test how psychological demands modulate communication across distributed neural networks.
